ABOUT ASHA FOUNDATION

Dedicated to Humanity & Social Equity

Established in 2012, Asha Foundation has been working tirelessly at the grassroots level to bring sustainable positive transformation in disadvantaged societies.

Our Mission

To eradicate poverty, hunger, and illiteracy by building accessible educational facilities, basic healthcare centers, and self-employment initiatives for marginalized communities regardless of caste, creed, or gender.

👁

Our Vision

A world where every human being has access to clean food, quality education, dignified health standards, and an equal opportunity to live with self-respect and prosperity.

Historical Journey

Milestones of Hope & Progress

How a small team of 5 passionate changemakers grew into a national movement.

2012
Foundation Inception in New Delhi

Registered under Section 8. Started our first weekend tuition center for 35 children in an urban resettlement colony.

2016
First 25 Rural Schools Completed

Expanded to rural Uttar Pradesh and Bihar, distributing 50,000 sets of free textbooks and constructing clean sanitation facilities.

2020
National Emergency Relief Mission

Delivered dry ration kits, oxygen cylinders, and emergency supplies to over 60,000 migrant families during crisis periods.

2023
Mobile Medical Vans Fleet Launched

Inaugurated 8 fully equipped mobile clinics conducting free diagnosis, maternal healthcare, and emergency deliveries in tribal belts.

2026
100,000+ Lives Impacted Milestone

Crossed 120 completed schools, 250k trees planted, and over 1,200 active field volunteers nationwide.
